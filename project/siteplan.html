Site Plan

Site Name: G&M Viajes

This name represents an enterprise that is dedicated to offering tourism services.

Purpose of the site

The site offers information about travel packages, such as available destinations, dates, prices, promotions, and what is included in the package. There will also be contact information for making reservations and there will also be information about the business owners.

Scenarios

  • What payment methods are available?
  • Can I bring my pet on excursions?

Color Schema

Color 1: orange-yellow #fff1d0 - Used for headings and accents.

Color 2: blue-violet #302b6e - Used for the main background of the site.

Tipography

Principal font: Arial, lato - It will be used for paragraphs and the main body of the document.

Secondary font: Roboto, sans-serif - It will be used for headers and titles.
